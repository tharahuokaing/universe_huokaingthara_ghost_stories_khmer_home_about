## 🎯 About the Module / អំពីម៉ូឌុល

**`universe_huokaingthara_ghost_stories_khmer_home_about`** is the dedicated "About" module for **និទានរឿងខ្មោចខ្មែរ (Ghost Stories Khmer)** within **UNIVERSE HUOKAING THARA Group**. It explains the series' mission of preserving Khmer folklore and sharing eerie experiences, highlights contributors and leadership, and shares official brand and logo copyright notices.

## ✨ Key Features / លក្ខណៈពិសេសចម្បង

* **🗂️ Interactive Section Tabs:** Switch smoothly between *All Sections*, *Mission & Craft*, *Story & Group Team*, and *Brand Updates*.
* **🕯️ Storytelling & Folklore Overview:** Highlights Khmer supernatural legends, real-life eerie encounters, midnight episodes, and bilingual digital reader features.
* **👥 Contributor & Governance Credits:** Recognizes storytelling contributor **Mr. Chorn Rothanak (លោក ជອນ រតនៈ)**, group leadership, and our elite programmers and developers.
* **🌐 Dual Language Switcher (Khmer / English):** Instant toggle supporting Khmer-only, English-only, or bilingual display modes.
* **🌙 Dark / Light Cyberpunk Theme:** Styled with the signature **UNIVERSE HUOKAING THARA** holographic scanline aesthetic and adaptive theme switching.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Architecture / បច្ចេកវិទ្យាប្រើប្រាស់

- **Frontend Core:** Semantic HTML5, CSS3 (CSS Grid, Flexbox, Custom Properties), Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+)
- **Typography:** Google Fonts (`Kantumruy Pro` for Khmer script, `Poppins` for English UI)
